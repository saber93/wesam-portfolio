04 / A PRACTICAL APPROACH TO FM

From understanding
to a working plan.

A proposed approach to supporting an owner’s maintenance function. The scope would be agreed around the portfolio, existing team and priorities.

START WITH THE ACTUAL CONDITION

Make the priorities visible.

Review representative properties, available asset records, recurring faults and current maintenance arrangements with the responsible team.

  • Asset condition and criticality review
  • Immediate maintenance risks and backlog
  • Existing team, contractors and AMC coverage
  • Prioritized action list for management review
TURN KNOWLEDGE INTO ROUTINE

Give the work a clear structure.

Support the development of practical maintenance plans, technical responsibilities and service expectations suited to the assets and resources.

  • Priority preventive maintenance schedules
  • Roles, technical competencies and staffing input
  • Work instructions and contractor expectations
  • Asset data and operational CMMS requirements
BUILD CONSISTENCY THROUGH FOLLOW-UP

Test, learn and strengthen the team.

Pilot agreed processes in a defined area, review the quality of completed work and help the team turn technical knowledge into consistent daily practice.

  • Practical technical training and mentoring
  • Completed-work and contractor quality reviews
  • PPM completion, backlog and repeat-fault reporting
  • Improvement priorities and handover recommendations
